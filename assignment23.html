Why This Design? - Q23 Justification

1. Font Pairing Chosen:

Poppins (Headings) - Bold, modern, attention-grabbing. Google Fonts me top resume font hai.
Inter (Body) - Super readable, professional, long text ke liye best. Both sans-serif so they pair well.

2. Colour Palette Chosen:

Primary #0F2C61 (Deep Navy): Trust, professionalism - Resume ke liye perfect.
Accent #FF6B35 (Orange): Energy, creativity - Highlights ke liye. Navy ke saath high contrast.
Background #F8F9FA (Light Grey): Eye-friendly, content ko pop karata hai.
Rule: 60-30-10 rule - 60% Light, 30% Navy, 10% Orange.
