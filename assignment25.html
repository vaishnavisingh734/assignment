Q25 - SHORT NOTE ON DESIGN CHOICES (Required for Submission)

1. Colour Palette Choice - Why Navy #0F2C61 + Orange #FF6B35?

Primary #0F2C61 Deep Navy: Professional, trust, academic. Resume/portfolio ke liye best. Dark colour header/footer ke liye use kiya taki content white cards me pop kare.

Accent #FF6B35 Orange: Energy, creativity, call-to-action. Navy ka complementary colour hai, high contrast deta hai (WCAG accessible). Buttons, highlights, left borders ke liye use kiya.

Background #F8F9FA Light Grey: Eye strain kam karta hai, pure white se better readability. 60-30-10 Rule follow kiya: 60% light background, 30% navy, 10% orange.

2. Font Pairing Choice - Why Poppins + Inter?

Heading Font: Poppins (700 Bold): Modern geometric sans-serif, headings ko strong aur memorable banata hai. Google Fonts me most used for portfolios.

Body Font: Inter (400 Regular): Designed for screens, super readable even at 14px. Long paragraphs ke liye best. Both fonts sans-serif family ke hain, isliye pair ache se hota hai.

Scale System: --fs-xs 12px labels, --fs-base 16px body (browser default), --fs-lg 24px sections, --fs-xl 32px hero. Consistent rhythm.

3. Design System Choice - Why CSS Variables (Design Tokens)?

Q24 ke tokens ko Q25 me reuse kiya: :root me saare colours, font sizes, spacing ek jagah define kiye. Benefit: Agar kal client bole "Orange ko red karo" to sirf --color-accent change karna hai, poori 25 pages update ho jayengi. Real companies (Google Material, Shopify Polaris) yahi karti hain.

4. Layout & Selector Choices

Q22 ke 4+ Selectors used: Universal (*) reset, Element (h2, nav), ID (#main-header), Class (.card, .btn), Attribute (input[type="text"]), Pseudo-class (nav a:hover, :focus). Requirement 4 tha, 6 use kiye.

Responsive: Grid-2 desktop me 2 columns, mobile me 1 column (@media 700px). Sticky nav for multi-page navigation (Q11 concept).

Evolution: Q20 = HTML-only, Q21 = lightly styled, Q23 = resume styled, Q24 = tokens, Q25 = Final complete styled system with all concepts merged.
